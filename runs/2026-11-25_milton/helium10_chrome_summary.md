## 1. Headline findings

1. **Milton is a real #1 in two sub-categories, built on low prices.**
   - Electric kettles: #1 brand with **36% of units and 26% of revenue**. Average selling price (ASP) is ₹687 vs ₹1,095 for the rest of the market.
   - Sandwich makers: #1 brand with **33% of units and 29% of revenue**.
2. **Revenue is concentrated in one product family.**
   - Across the 46 Milton ASINs captured, Milton does about **₹15.1 Cr per 30 days** (~150k units).
   - The Rapid kettle (B0F54GKQ38) alone is **27.5%** of that, and the top 3 ASINs are 40.5%.
   - Rapid and Champ (B0D3XVTRRY) sit in the same listing family. They share parent-level reviews (10,842) and an identical keyword set.
3. **Mid-tier or weak beyond the core.**
   - Rice & pasta / multi-cook: #3 brand with 18% of units, all from one ASIN (Champ).
   - Egg boilers: #2 behind AGARO with 17% of units.
   - Gas stoves: #5 with 5.7%, and sales are down 10% year on year while the market grew 49%.
   - Air fryers: #18 with 0.9%.
4. **Growth is strong where Milton is already strong.** Unit-weighted sales year on year:
   - Kettles: +248% vs +34% for the market.
   - Egg boilers: +270% vs +109%.
   - Multi-cook: +315% vs +60%.
   - Milton's listings are also younger. Kettle listings average 31 months vs 52 for competitors, so this is recent momentum, not legacy share.
5. **The brand name is not an asset for appliances on Amazon.**
   - "milton" has ~937k monthly searches, but nearly all of it is bottles and flasks (housewares). Milton appliance ASINs rank only #94 to #284 on it.
   - Appliance-intent "milton …" terms add up to ~106k searches, about **7.5% of all "milton" search volume**.
   - Demand is captured on generic terms ("electric kettle", "sandwich maker", "gas stove"), not through brand pull.
6. **Distribution is mostly first-party.** HYDRO VALVES. sells **86% of Milton revenue**. The rest comes from RetailEZ (7.9%), DK HOMES (5.7%) and Cloudtail. About 91% of Milton units ship via FBA.
7. **Ratings are at the market average, with no premium.** Milton's unit-weighted rating is 4.0 to 4.2, in line with competitors in every segment. Gas stoves (4.0 vs 3.9) and multi-cook (4.0 vs 3.8) are slightly above.

## 5. Method

- **Brand matching:** `Brand` equal to "milton", case-insensitive. All Milton rows in scope are kitchen appliances. Sellers are HYDRO VALVES., RetailEZ, DK HOMES, Cloudtail and HOME FUMES.
- **Share:** brand ASIN Sales (or ASIN Revenue) divided by the total across all rows in the file.
- **ASP:** revenue divided by units.
- **Ratings, YoY and listing age:** weighted by ASIN units.
- **HHI:** sum of squared brand unit shares, in percent (0 to 10,000).
- **Cerebro rank buckets:** organic rank between 1 and 10 counts as "top 10".
- **Keyword-gap lists:** taken from the multi-ASIN file, keywords with search volume of at least 500 that contain the category stem.
- **Milton total (₹15.1 Cr, 150k units):** the unique Milton ASINs across all 8 Black Box files (46 ASINs). It is **not** total brand sales. Milton ASINs outside these sub-categories or below each file's minimum are excluded.

## 6. Caveats

1. **Kettles at minimum 50 is not the top 200.**
   - Helium 10 exports the first 200 rows in its default order, not by sales.
   - The file goes down to 50 units but misses 28 of the 183 ASINs that sell 101 or more.
   - Use the min-100 file. The gas min-10 file does contain all of the min-100 rows.
2. **Market totals are floors.**
   - Each segment excludes ASINs below its minimum.
   - Small Kitchen Appliances is cut at 1,844 units, so the egg-boiler segment shows only the head of that market.
3. **Seasonality.**
   - The data is the 30 days to 25 Nov 2026, the festive peak.
   - `Sales Trend (90 days)` values of +400–600% are seasonal, not structural. Year-on-year is the better growth signal.
   - Don't multiply by 12.
4. **Helium 10 estimates are modelled from BSR,** not reported sales. Use them for relative position more than absolute ₹.
5. **Rapid and Champ (B0F54GKQ38 / B0D3XVTRRY) are one listing family.** Their keyword sets are identical, but organic ranks differ on 469 keywords. Don't double-count their search volume.
6. **Cerebro search volume is noisy for broad stems.**
   - The egg stem picks up grocery "eggs" terms. Those were excluded from the conclusions.
   - "milton" brand search mixes housewares and appliances.
7. **Search budget.** The Cerebro allowance used this run was 20 of 100 (60 left this month). A multi-ASIN run costs one search per ASIN.

## 7. Suggested checks for the Brand Fit Screen

- **Concentration risk:** one product family drives about 34% of captured revenue (Rapid plus Champ ≈ ₹5.15 Cr of ₹15.1 Cr). Test whether the screen penalises this.
- **Category quality:** Milton's leadership is in low-ASP categories (kettles, sandwich makers, egg boilers, all under about ₹1,200). In the larger-ticket categories (gas stoves, air fryers) it is minor. Weight shares by category size: air fryers alone are ₹123 Cr per 30 days vs ₹25 Cr for kettles.
- **Brand pull:** appliance-intent brand search is about 7.5% of "milton" search volume. The brand name helps less than its awareness suggests.
- **Data gaps to fill elsewhere:**
  - Keepa for price history and discount depth.
  - Off-Amazon channels (Flipkart, q-commerce via GobbleCube, D2C).
  - Return and complaint rates, which the outside-in data can't see.
